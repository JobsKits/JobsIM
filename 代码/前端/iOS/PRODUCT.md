# JobsIM iOS

Native iPhone and iPad client for the JobsIM self-hosted daily messaging product. Stack is explicitly Swift/UIKit, JobsByPods, SnapKit, JobsNetworking and GRDB/system SQLite. The explicit parent brief supplies product context in this delegated implementation; no separate interview is needed here.

People connect their own verified fixed server, register or recover without email, and use conversations and groups. Formal data, drafts, media and Outbox remain bound to account and trusted connection profile. An explicitly entered demo has its own namespace and a persistent return action. No RTC, payment, maps, E2EE or public SDK is enabled.

Success is real service-backed interactions and durable local state with truthful failure/unknown states. Full I requirements and the 243 acceptance items are broader than this first code slice; unexecuted items remain unaccepted. No native hardware result is inferred from a simulator build.
